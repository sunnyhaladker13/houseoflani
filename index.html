The Philosophy

House of Lani is a space designed for how life actually moves.

From intentional movement and deep recovery to moments of play and pause, Lani brings it all together under one roof.

Over the past year, we've imagined, built, and refined every detail—creating a space that feels as good as it functions and bringing every need of our body together to function at its optimal state.

The Symphony of Five

Our Offerings

01

Pilates

We bring this art that blends strength and low impact movement by focusing on control, alignment, and breath. Designed to build deep core strength, improve posture, increase mobility, aid rehabilitation and support both everyday movement and athletic performance. Let’s move the body in a balanced, precise and intentional way.

02

Yoga

Rooted in ancient traditions and adapted for modern life, yoga encourages conscious movement and stillness—helping improve center, flexibility, balance, and nervous system health. Accessible to all levels, yoga meets you where you are, offering a sense of calm that extends beyond the mat.

03

Pickleball

Pickleball, but make it social. Fast rallies, easy rules, lots of laughs. Come for the movement, stay for the people. Whether you’re new or a pro, our courts are made for connection, friendly competition, and feel-good energy. At Lani, pickleball isn’t just a game—it’s how we come together.

04

Recovery

Recovery is where the real progress happens. It’s the reset your body needs after movement, stress, and everyday life. Through targeted therapy, we help reduce soreness, improve circulation, and support faster muscle repair—so you can move better, feel stronger, and come back refreshed.

Routine as Ritual

Transform your daily routine into a practice of self-care. A space to breathe, move, and just be.
